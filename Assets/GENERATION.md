## App icon prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: final macOS app icon for Codex Switch, an account routing utility. ONE icon only, no text. Square canvas, genuine transparent background outside a rounded-square tile that fills 92% of canvas. Dark graphite tile, restrained matte enamel depth and subtle rim. Center a distinctive geometric S routing monogram made from two opposing interlocking folded bands with generous separation, confident 45-degree chamfers and slightly rounded corners. Upper band warm ivory, lower band pale jade. Mark fills about 60% of tile, thick solid forms, optically balanced, legible at 24px. Precise crafted contemporary Mac utility aesthetic, essentially flat frontal view. No standard bidirectional arrows, thin strokes, circuitry, sparkles, AI brain, OpenAI knot, wordmark, labels, mockups or watermark.
